GOALS OF THE
PRODUCT
The primary goal of the AI-Enabled
Clinical Research Search Tool is to
enhance research efficiency and foster
patient engagement in clinical trials. By
creating an advanced digital platform that
integrates structured and unstructured
data, our tool will empower stakeholders—
patients, clinicians, researchers, and CROs
—to seamlessly discover, filter, and analyze
global clinical trials. With intuitive user
experience (UX) design and advanced
natural language processing (NLP), the
tool will bridge the information gap in
clinical research. It aims to facilitate
informed decision-making and improve
trial recruitment by allowing users to easily
identify relevant trials based on specific
criteria. In doing so, we aspire to not only
streamline the research process but also
enhance patient outcomes through
increased participation in suitable clinical
studies.Enhance research efficiency
