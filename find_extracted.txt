iClinicalAI FIND

Objective

An AI-assisted Clinical Trial Search, Registry Intelligence, Research Discovery, and Academic Research Support System designed to help Clinical Research Professionals, CROs, Pharma Companies, Academic Institutions, Research Scholars, Medical Students, Pharmacy Students, Biotechnology Researchers, and Universities identify, analyze, compare, and monitor clinical trials while supporting research and learning activities.

Important

This module is NOT intended to replace ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed, Google Scholar, or other research databases.

Instead, it acts as an intelligent search, benchmarking, competitive intelligence, research discovery, and academic support assistant that helps users quickly identify relevant studies, discover research opportunities, analyze competitors, and generate actionable insights.
