
Scope of Work
iClinicalAi Platform – MVP Development
1. Project Objective
To develop an AI-assisted clinical research automation platform that supports clinical trial design, clinical trial search, clinical data management, biostatistics, safety, recruitment, prediction, and documentation support.
The platform will act as an Ai co-pilot for clinical research teams, CROs, sponsors, SMEs, data managers, biostatisticians, safety teams, and medical writers.
This scope is based on the iClinicalAi product concept covering DESIGN, RECRUIT, MANAGE, ANALYSE, SAFETY, PREDICT, and FIND modules.
