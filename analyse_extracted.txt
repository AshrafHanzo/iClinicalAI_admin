iClinicalAI ANALYSE
An Objective
An AI-assisted Biostatistics, Statistical Programming, and Clinical Analytics Support System designed to help Biostatisticians, Statistical Programmers, Clinical Data Scientists, Medical Writers, Clinical Researchers, CROs, and Sponsors streamline statistical planning, analysis review, interpretation, and reporting activities.
Important: This module is NOT intended to replace qualified Biostatisticians, Statistical Programmers, or validated statistical software. Instead, it acts as an intelligent analytics assistant that helps users review study data, generate analysis documentation, prepare reporting structures, interpret results, and accelerate statistical deliverables.
