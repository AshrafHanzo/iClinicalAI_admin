iClinicalAI SAFETY
An Objective
An AI-assisted Pharmacovigilance, Drug Safety, and Medical Review Support System designed to help Pharmacovigilance Professionals, Drug Safety Associates, Medical Reviewers, Medical Monitors, CROs, Sponsors, and Clinical Research Organizations streamline safety case review, narrative preparation, safety analysis, and aggregate reporting activities.
Important: This module is NOT intended to replace qualified Pharmacovigilance professionals, Medical Reviewers, or Regulatory Safety Experts. Instead, it serves as an intelligent safety assistant that supports case review, narrative drafting, safety signal review, aggregate safety analysis, and medical review activities while requiring appropriate expert oversight.
